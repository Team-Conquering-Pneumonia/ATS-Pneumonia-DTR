About This Work

Dynamic treatment regimes (DTRs) personalize antibiotic selection based on a patient's pneumonia severity, viral etiology, and comorbidity profile. We used outcome-weighted learning on a large VA pneumonia cohort to estimate tree-based DTRs optimizing 30-day survival.
